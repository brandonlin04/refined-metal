About Refined Metal

Refined Metal is a New York–based manufacturer delivering high-quality steel products at competitive prices. We specialize in roll-formed studs, tracks, joists, and decking, while also offering durable doors, railings, and custom solutions.

By cutting out the middleman and serving contractors directly, we provide faster lead times, flexible options, and the reliability you need to keep projects moving forward.

Our manufacturing facility in College Point, New York, allows us to maintain strict quality control while keeping costs competitive. We understand that construction projects require timely delivery and reliable materials.

Our steel solutions for construction are backed by ICC-ES certifications, ensuring they meet or exceed industry standards. Learn more about our engineering certifications.
