Smart Studs - Steel Framing Members

Precision-engineered Smart Studs for modern construction and framing applications. Lightweight, durable cold-formed steel framing members perfect for smart and drywall systems.

Refined Metal Smart Studs

Refined Metal Smart Studs are precision-engineered cold-formed steel framing members designed for both smart and drywall applications. Manufactured under strict ICC-ES quality control programs, our studs comply with the 2024 and 2021 IBC/IRC requirements, ensuring reliability in residential, commercial, and industrial construction.

Our studs are available in multiple gauges, web depths, and flange sizes to meet project-specific load demands. With pre-punched knockout options for electrical, plumbing, and mechanical runs, Refined Metal studs deliver both strength and efficiency on the jobsite. Produced from galvanized steel coils, our products exceed ASTM A1003 and A653 requirements, providing excellent corrosion resistance and long-term durability.

Smart Studs

Smart Studs are designed for load-bearing applications including curtain walls, floor joists, headers, roof trusses, and shear walls. Manufactured from high-strength steel (minimum Grade 33 ksi for light gauges and Grade 50 ksi for heavy gauges), these studs meet AISI S240 standards for Cold-Formed Steel Smart Framing.

Perfect for schools, hospitals, mid-rise buildings, and heavy-duty framing assemblies requiring long-span and high-strength performance. Available in gauges 20S through 12 (33 to 97 mils) with minimum G60 coating, with G90 available for enhanced protection.

Drywall Studs

Drywall Studs are designed for interior and exterior non-load-bearing walls, partitions, and architectural features. These studs provide excellent dimensional stability and ease of installation for standard framing applications.

Ideal for interior walls, demising walls, and architectural features where smart loads are not a concern. Features pre-punched knockouts for easy utility installation and consistent spacing for optimal efficiency.

EQ Smart Studs

EQ Smart Studs are earthquake-resistant studs engineered for seismic applications. Enhanced design provides superior performance in earthquake-prone regions with specialized connections and reinforcement.

Our EQ studs meet the most stringent seismic design requirements and are tested to withstand significant lateral forces. Essential for construction in seismic zones and high-wind areas where smart integrity is paramount.
